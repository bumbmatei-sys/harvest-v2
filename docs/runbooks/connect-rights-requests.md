# Staff handling of Connect rights requests

Documentation only, UNAPPROVED, not activated. HC-02 decisions remain UNAPPROVED. Production collection and delivery are hard-disabled, and production migrations 0031+ are unapplied. This runbook is for synthetic rehearsal and review; it does not authorize production SQL, deployment, exports or messages. Acceptance: [HC-14](../acceptance/hc-14.md).

1. **Receive and limit access.** Route the request to the ministry's designated privacy owner/admin. Record a request reference, received date, requested purpose/action and assigned handler in the restricted request register. Do not put contact details, request text, identity documents or religious information in logs, case metadata or broad CRM notes. The operational register, handler, escalation channel and legally reviewed deadlines must be selected before use.
2. **Verify proportionately.** A signed-in person's existing verified account and opaque response handle support their own Connect rights. For staff-assisted requests, use a previously verified relationship or an independently verified existing contact method. Do not rely on a supplied name/email match alone, ask for a password/token, or disclose whether a response exists to an unverified requester. If identity or authority remains uncertain, escalate to the privacy handler without disclosing data. Document only verification method and request reference in the restricted register. The final verification policy requires privacy/security review.
3. **Confirm scope.** Distinguish follow-up withdrawal, newsletter withdrawal, account/response rights and full contact erasure. Do not convert a withdrawal into deletion or infer a grant from another purpose. For a follow-up opt-out, every non-viewer ministry member can record the withdrawal through the existing CRM operation with required source, evidence and date. Only owner/admin can attest grants. Use minimal factual evidence and a request reference; never paste identity documents or counseling content into the ledger. Staff pending-response operations are owner/admin only; an associated response belongs to the subject-rights path, not the pending-response staff endpoint.
4. **Act within the existing permissions.** In synthetic rehearsal, use the existing staff CRM withdrawal or the verified subject-rights operation. HC-14 atomically withdraws non-terminal follow-up cases for the contact across channels, clears assignments and records an actorless system audit. Closed cases stay closed; a later grant does not reopen a withdrawn case. For erasure, an owner/admin uses the existing erasure operation; the case is withdrawn/audited then deleted with the scrubbed response. Do not bypass the application with ad hoc SQL or revive an erased identity. Response-only erasure and whole-contact erasure have different scopes.
5. **Check completion.** Under an owner/admin identity verify the intended case states and audit entries, and confirm an assigned member no longer sees the withdrawn case. Verify unrelated purposes and other ministries were unaffected. An error rolls back the transaction: a failed attempt is not completed work. Escalate safe reason/status codes and the request reference; do not copy database error text or personal data into logs. Denial logging is not implemented.
6. **Access requests and exceptions.** There is no staff DSAR export workflow in HC-14. Do not use a broad CRM export as a response. Have the privacy handler review authority, third-party information, scope, secure delivery and applicable deadlines before any response. Retention exceptions, disputes, safeguarding or legal holds require a reviewed procedure; this document establishes none.
7. **Close the request.** Record outcome, completion date, handler and verification method in the restricted register. Any confirmation must use an independently selected secure channel after the required permissions and delivery decisions; no real message is authorized by this task. Keep proof of rehearsal separate from independent review. Request-register retention, audit retention and incident escalation remain unresolved activation prerequisites.

## Newsletter suppression

A keyed newsletter address digest remains personal data. CRM contact erasure detaches delivery links and clears attempted address digests once the erased subscriber has no canonical successor. The newsletter delivery purge removes any remaining individual delivery digests at 90 days and preserves anonymous counts. A fresh verified signup deletes eligible unsubscribe records for the affected scope across configured key versions. Hard bounce and complaint digests survive CRM erasure to prevent sending to a recreated address.

For a verified request specifically seeking erasure of a retained suppression record, the privacy owner must document whether the service still has a basis to keep the block. An approved deletion would match the verified address inside the ministry across active and historical key versions, delete only the authorized scope and reason, and record the outcome and count in the restricted rights register. The document, logs and register must exclude addresses, digests and keys. The exceptional deletion mechanism and criteria are UNAPPROVED and require privacy and founder sign-off before activation.
