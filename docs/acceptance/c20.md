## Deferred and residual

1. **Applications and invitations are not counted.** Only an open join that creates the membership in the same request counts; an application approved later, or an invitation redemption, is not attributed (no person link can carry it).
2. **Leave and rejoin counts again.** A re-entry updates the existing membership with a new `joined_at`, which the counter cannot tell apart from a first join without a person link. Bounded by deliberate effort and covered by the small-count rule.
3. **Sign-up path.** The `?qr=` return survives `/sign-in`; if a brand-new person goes through a flow that does not return to the community page, their join is simply not counted.
4. **Timing correlation.** Ministry staff can see community member join times and the QR count; with five or more joins the figure is shown. The count has no time stamp and small counts are withheld, but a staff member polling both could still guess. Recorded for review; a coarser display (for example rounding) is a founder choice.
5. **Recency signal.** "Recent activity" is the listing's last public change, not member activity; an activity signal needs the fraud and quality controls PRD 8A.3 asks for first.
6. **Courses and products in the QR picker.** Only a community can be recommended on the QR page (scope of this slice).
