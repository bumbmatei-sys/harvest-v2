## Consent safety

Zod validates the occurrence timestamp against the current clock on every parse, allowing at most five minutes of skew (including ISO offsets). The date picker disables later dates; the shadcn time Input has a `max` for the latest permitted UTC day. Submission also checks the combined UTC instant. PostgreSQL enforces `occurred_at <= recorded_at + interval '5 minutes'` independently of the API.

Contact detail returns both the history and effective consent per purpose/channel. Both use descending `recorded_at`, then `occurred_at`, then ID for stable ties. The Marketing preferences card uses the effective result. A withdrawal recorded after a grant wins even when its occurrence is backdated or the grant's occurrence is within the future skew allowance. No marketing permission is inferred from contact creation.

## Erasure and audit retention

Erasure still rekeys consent IDs, detaches contact/actor/channel, clears source/evidence, and freezes the contact tombstone. It now coarsens `recorded_at`, `occurred_at` and `redacted_at` to UTC day boundaries. The redaction trigger permits only these exact timestamp transformations, while preserving tenant, status, purpose and notice version. DELETE/TRUNCATE, repeated redaction and app-role UPDATE remain forbidden.

For permitted clock skew across midnight, the occurrence day is clamped to the recording day. Otherwise day truncation could turn a two-minute difference into a 24-hour difference and violate the same CHECK. This edge case is covered explicitly. Day-level values intentionally lose event-time precision.

Audit rows remain entirely append-only, including their existing `metadata.channel`; no audit permissions, triggers, payloads or timestamps are modified. Removing the precise timestamps from anonymous consent is sufficient to break the reviewed exact timestamp joins, so an audit-retention exception is unnecessary. The erasure test joins every redacted row's three timestamps against audit creation times within the workspace and asserts zero matches, while keeping the surviving facts and unrelated contacts intact. This tests the reviewed exact-timestamp linkage; day-level correlations are inherently coarser and are not a claim of anonymity against every possible external dataset.
