# CRM-07 acceptance record: contact association events with privacy and consent controls

Date: 2026-11-06. Scope: PRD v2.5 §5A.4 CRM-07: "Zernio/form/contact association events with privacy/consent controls". Activity from Zernio, forms and other touchpoints is linked to the right person (and optionally a company). It is shown on their timelines and stored only while the person's consent for that purpose allows it.

**Not in scope:** email inbox sync, calendar sync, CRM-06 import/export/merge code (#21), CRM-08.

## Decisions

1. **One ledger, no new history store.**
   - Each stored association is an `association.record` row in `audit_logs`, written with the content row and a `crm.association.recorded` outbox event in one transaction.
   - The content (summary and raw payload) lives in `crm_associations`, exactly as note text lives in `crm_notes`.
   - The timeline reads the ledger row and joins the content, so the cursor (`(created_at, id) < cursor`, 50 per page, `<UTC ISO µs>_<uuid>`) and paging are unchanged.
2. **A new timeline kind.** `TimelineItem` gains `{ kind: "association" }` (with `action: "association.record"`), and `timelineActions` gains `association.record`. It shows on the person's timeline, and on the company's timeline when a company is linked. Deals do not take associations in this slice.
3. **Consent is per purpose, never bundled.**
   - The existing append-only consent ledger gains three purposes: `messaging`, `form_follow_up` and `newsletter`. Each is recorded separately, with when (`occurred_at` and `recorded_at`) and how (`source`, `evidence`).
   - An association needs an **effective** opt-in for its purpose:
     - any outstanding withdrawal for that purpose (on any channel, with no later grant on both clocks) wins;
     - otherwise the latest grant allows.
   - Without that, **nothing** is stored: no content, no ledger row and no outbox event. The caller gets `{ stored: false, reason: "consent_missing" | "consent_withdrawn" }`.
   - The same rule is enforced in SQL by `guard_crm_association`, which locks the person row so a concurrent withdrawal serializes.
   - Withdrawing stops new associations; existing ones are kept (see follow-ups).
4. **Matching.**
   - **People:** by explicit `contactId`, or by tenant-unique normalized email, phone or handle. Identifiers that point to two people give a 409; no match gives `no_match` (nothing stored). Names never match.
   - **Companies:** by id, or by normalized domain.
   - **Redelivery:** the same `(source, externalId)` is idempotent.
5. **Security.**
   - `crm_associations` has `tenant_id`, composite FKs (contact, company, consent), and ENABLE + FORCE RLS with a `harvest_app`-only policy.
   - The app gets SELECT and INSERT grants only (no UPDATE or DELETE).
   - `erasure_owner_tenant` is granted for the erasure definer.
   - Services filter by `tenant_id` explicitly (for example `loadAssociations`).
   - Recording needs `contacts:write`. Reading follows the record (`contacts:read` / `companies:read`): unknown record 404, another workspace 403, a foreign id 404.
6. **Erasure** (`erase_contact`, staged):
   - deletes the person's associations;
   - scrubs (summary `''`, payload `{}`, `redacted_at`) every other association whose summary or raw payload contains the person's **email** (case-insensitive) or **phone** (digits only, at least 7 digits);
   - strips `contactId` from `association.*` ledger rows (audit guard extended exactly like `task.*` and `note.*`).

   The outbox `contactId` scrub from CRM-05 already covers `crm.association.recorded`.
7. **Merge.** `crm_associations.contact_id` can change only inside the merge definer: the owner of `merge_contacts` with a matching `app.merge_source`, failing closed via `to_regprocedure` and `coalesce`. `merge_contacts` exists only in #21, so the step that moves associations to the survivor lands with migration 0024. Its test (`crm-associations.test.ts`, "moves associations to the surviving person…") is written now and **skips itself until `merge_contacts` exists**.
8. **UI** (shadcn only; Twenty-like record page and timeline behaviour, independently written):
   - a **Contact permissions** card on the person page: one row per purpose with state, when and how, and a single "Record opt-in" or "Record withdrawal" action that opens the existing consent form preset to that purpose;
   - **Log activity** (source, what happened, purpose, when in Europe/Berlin, summary, reference). It explains when nothing was saved and why;
   - association entries on person and company timelines;
   - at least 44 px tap targets on mobile for every new control and for "Show older activity";
   - all copy in `messages/en.json`.

## Parked follow-ups

- **Zernio adapter and public forms.** Neither exists on main yet (only a synthetic webhook). `recordAssociation` and `POST /crm/associations` are the contract they will call.
- **Merge move** of associations and migration 0024, after #21.
- **Withdrawal does not delete existing associations.** A "withdraw and delete" choice, and retention for raw payloads, need a product decision.
- **Phone text matching** compares the full international digits. A number written in national format without the country code is not matched.
- **No deal associations**, and no editing or deleting of a single association by users.
- Existing pre-CRM-07 controls (for example the record tabs) were not re-measured for the 44 px rule; only new and touched controls were.
