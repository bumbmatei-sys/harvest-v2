# Harvest Connect threat model and data processing inventory

Date: 2026-11-06. Status: HC-01 design for review; legal/privacy and security signoffs pending. No controls below are claimed implemented by this documentation PR.

Sources: [Connect PRD](../prd/harvest-connect-qr-to-discipleship.md) §§2–3, 5, 7–12, 16–19; [ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-harvest-connect.md); [slice plan](../prd/harvest-connect-slices.md); [acceptance record](../acceptance/harvest-connect.md). This is a processing/design inventory for review, not a completed DPIA or legal determination. There was no existing `docs/security/` convention at the baseline.

## Assets, actors and trust boundaries

Protect contact methods, purpose/notice evidence, response content, account claims, community memberships, case assignments/referrals, staff/device identity and durable delivery payloads. Ministry context and membership can themselves reveal sensitive information even without an explicit belief field. Religious classifications and detailed counseling notes are off by default.

Actors include anonymous visitors, malicious submitters, free members, campaign organizers, volunteers, approved counselors, church liaisons, privacy administrators, platform operators and external delivery providers. An authenticated employee or partner church can still exceed their permitted purpose.

Boundaries are: public QR/browser → constrained public intake; verified platform identity → explicit account claim; staff session → verified workspace/campaign; workspace → assignment/recipient scope; account → community admission; application transaction → jobs/providers; staff device → offline sync. Cross-workspace membership does not grant origin-campaign access. Campaign finance has independent permissions and never consumes religious responses; donor identity is not pastoral permission.

## Threats and required controls

| Threat / misuse | Required control | Future proof / owning slices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unconsented sharing across churches: partner edits church/tenant IDs or reads origin records | FORCE RLS, tenant-composite parents, recipient-specific disclosure grant with fields/purpose/expiry; deny partner-wide rosters and onward referrals without a fresh choice | Two tenants and two churches, both directions; forged parent and recipient IDs denied; HC-02, HC-18 |
| Purpose creep: follow-up or an invitation becomes marketing or donation targeting | Separate unchecked choices and ledger purposes; marketing subscriptions isolated; live permission checks in every consumer; no belief-based advertising/AI training | Invitation-only/follow-up-only never subscribes or solicits; HC-05–HC-08, HC-13–HC-14 |
| Roster export through dashboards, CSV, APIs or small cohort counts | Aggregate-only organizer dashboards and exports by default; minimum cohort suppression and controls against differencing; any exceptional person export needs separate authorization, reason, audit and legal review | No raw response/drill-through; small cohorts suppressed; unauthorized exports denied; HC-15–HC-16 |
| Counselor over-access after reassignment or staff removal | Assignment-scoped server and DB policies, screened live staff membership, minimal projections, audited views and narrowly approved elevation; coordinator has only necessary operational fields | Unassigned counselor denied even within same tenant; former assignee and revoked staff denied; HC-02, HC-13 |
| QR/account enumeration or leaked claim links | Unguessable public QR codes reveal public metadata only; no sequential record lookup; separate short-lived single-use hashed claim tokens; safe uniform failures and activation replies; no PII/tokens in URLs beyond opaque capability | Guessing cannot expose submissions or account existence; replay/expiry/disabled QR tests; HC-03–HC-04, HC-07, HC-16 |
| Submitted address claims another person's identity or retry duplicates people/cases | Verify control and authorized claim before CRM association; tenant identifier uniqueness, stable submission/delivery keys; no name/email-only automatic merge | Forged association denied; existing account confirms; concurrent retries yield one intended effect; HC-05–HC-07 |
| Withdrawal/erasure leaves identifying jobs, outbox, DLQ or replay data | Reference-only events, controlled erasure across payloads and source rows, cancellation and permission recheck immediately before send, provider/recipient propagation | Inspect durable payloads after erasure; race revoke/dispatch and replay; fresh and upgraded DBs; HC-02, every producer, HC-14 |
| Public intake bypasses tenant checks or campaign form changes hide consent | Resolve authoritative tenant from active published source; constrained public access; immutable published form/notice versions; server-validated presets; transactional writes | Caller-supplied tenant/contact IDs ignored or denied; unpublished source and altered notice rejected; HC-02–HC-05 |
| QR burst, spam or malicious free text disrupts care and leaks into logs | Payload limits, schema validation, idempotency, adaptive abuse controls respecting venue/shared IPs, sanitized output and minimal diagnostic codes; no response bodies in analytics/logs | Crowd/load and abuse tests; no raw inputs in logs or generic events; HC-05, HC-16 |
| Lost assisted device, impersonation or stale offline authorization | Time-limited scoped staff sessions; person's own account verification; encrypted expiring queue only under approved device policy; revoke device, reauthorize sync and erase local data after acknowledgment | Lost device cannot sync/read; duplicate/offline conflict tests; no volunteer-issued permanent identity; HC-19–HC-20 |
| Community discovery leaks private membership, notes or unmoderated groups | Owner opt-in and moderation for listing; community authorization for join/content; directory and campaign responder lists isolated; safe ranking without response-derived beliefs | Private groups excluded; membership in A does not reveal B or origin response; HC-09–HC-12 |
| Privileged troubleshooting, providers or restored backups expose sensitive data | No routine operator content access; narrowly audited elevation, encrypted storage/transport, provider minimization, secret/token redaction, reviewed retention and deletion replay after restore | Access/retention review, provider deletion evidence and restore rehearsal before release; HC-14, HC-16 |

RLS complements server authorization under [ADR 0003](../decisions/0003-rls-tenancy.md); it does not protect against arbitrary SQL from a fully compromised server. Assignment checks and public access paths must be designed as explicit capabilities, not broad service-role bypasses. Encryption does not authorize a disclosure.

## Processing inventory by PRD journey J1–J8

All fields are conditional on the requested action. Common purpose evidence is the minimal subject/response reference, selected purpose and channel, notice/form version, capture time, source and grant/withdrawal state; referrals also name recipient, disclosed field set and expiry. Evidence is not a substitute for a reviewed lawful basis. No birth date, full address, denomination, testimony or belief classification is required by default. Collection of optional sensitive text/classification needs separate approved safeguards.

| Journey / purpose | Minimum personal data categories and fields | Processing owner / allowed recipients | Retention, rights and exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J1 — Scan and guest resources | No submitted personal fields or person-level religious response. Service necessarily handles transient network/security metadata; if retained for abuse defense, minimize IP-derived keys, timestamps and request codes separately from aggregate campaign/locale counts | Public-resource service; restricted security operations, organizer sees safe aggregates only | No CRM contact or person-level tracking from a view; no third-party trackers. Security-log expiry and aggregate thresholds need policy approval |
| J2 — Optional response | Selected purpose, language, optional preferred name; email **or** phone only for the chosen supported contact channel; purpose evidence and submission key. Availability/city only if needed and volunteered | Restricted campaign intake and scoped follow-up team when requested; marketing owner only for its independently granted purpose | Short-lived restricted pending response until verified association or expiry; withdrawal/erasure covers contact methods and evidence. Free text off by default; no inferred belief |
| J3 — Invitation and activation | Requested delivery address, token hash/expiry/use state, verified user/response reference; account display name and language, optional city; existing auth's minimal verification/security records | Authentication/claim service; chosen delivery provider gets only destination and neutral invitation; no partner church | Expire unused claims and abandoned intake; erase identifiers/payloads under relevant lifecycle. Never store/log raw claim tokens; no religious-status profile or compulsory workspace |
| J4 — Community discovery | Public browsing needs no identity; authenticated filters use language and optional self-declared region/interests. User/community IDs, join/request choice and time only when chosen | Community directory; selected community admission/moderation team gets needed membership/request fields | Prefer transient search filters; retain membership per reviewed policy and clear on leave/rights request as applicable. No origin response disclosure, belief inference or cross-community membership visibility |
| J5 — Welcome and courses | Chosen membership/user reference, permitted course/progress reference if used, notification preferences; personal care request only if explicitly made | Selected Community/Courses owners and authorized moderators; care team only for a requested scoped case | Progress/content retention and leave/delete behavior need review. No automatic counseling case, conversion marker or copy of private content into CRM notes |
| J6 — Church discovery and referral | Voluntary city/map search, transient device position only with permission; selected church ID, specifically approved name/contact fields, recipient evidence, referral status/time | Directory for search; only selected approved liaison receives the disclosure envelope | Do not persist precise location by default; revoke future contact and notify recipient. Referral expiry, recipient deletion/retention duties and sharing agreements pending |
| J7 — Staff-assisted response | J2 fields and affirmative choices plus staff ID, minimal device/session reference and capture/sync times for accountability | Time-limited approved volunteer intake; then same purpose-scoped recipients as J2; restricted audit administrator | No permanent account impersonation. Offline storage disabled until encrypted expiry/revocation policy passes; clear after acknowledgment, expire unsynced data |
| J8 — Campaign/pastor follow-up | Case/response/contact reference, permitted name/contact channel, language, volunteered availability, current permission, assignee, due/status, attempt channel/time/disposition | Coordinator's minimal assignment view; current approved counselor or authorized selected church; organizer receives aggregates only | Stop on withdrawal and remove identifying data on erasure/expiry, including queues. Detailed notes require separate elevation/policy; no generic CRM religious tags, bulk roster or marketing reuse |

Campaign/source IDs are operational metadata but become personal when linked to a person. Event and job references, idempotency keys, hashes and evidence can also remain identifying; inventory and erase them accordingly. Aggregate counts qualify for retention only when no person can reasonably be singled out, including through small groups or repeated queries.

## Storage, delivery and rights handling

- Reuse CRM contacts/identifiers/consents after verified association. Pending guest intake is restricted staging with a defined expiry, never a second contact directory. Published notice versions preserve what was presented without retaining unnecessary personal text.
- Connect's response/contact-method/claim/case/referral records, notifications, outbox/jobs and retry/replay material must participate in the controlled contact-erasure path. Cover unclaimed guest erasure as well as linked contacts. This carries the CRM-05 B1 payload-survival lesson in [ADR 0011](../decisions/0011-harvest-connect.md#withdrawal-erasure-and-durable-payloads); no existing branch's fix is certified here.
- Separate withdrawal from deletion: cancel further use for the revoked purpose/recipient immediately; retain only justified evidence under a reviewed policy. Reassignment, retries and provider callbacks must not restore permission. An accepted provider send cannot be recalled by a database rollback; record the race and propagate suppression.
- Log record access/assignment/export/override and purpose changes with minimal non-content metadata. A controlled redaction exception must also handle identifying audit references; normal writers cannot alter audit history or durable payloads.
- Review every cache/search projection, approved export, provider and offline copy; document deletion acknowledgment, backup expiry and suppression on restore. Account-wide rights requests reach relevant workspace/community owners without exposing unrelated records to the requesting organizer.
- Finance maintains its own donor/transaction processing inventory and lawful retention under PRD §21. Erasure of outreach data must not silently delete legally required finance records or expose donor identity to counselors.
