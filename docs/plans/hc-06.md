## Outcome and founder decision

When someone completes the existing verified campaign invitation/account claim, automatically create or reuse their contact in the originating Organization's CRM. Include account-only requests. **Do not add a separate CRM approval dialog, checkbox or confirmation step.** Matei explicitly chose this behavior during planning on 8 November.

The existing account claim remains an affirmative account action. Explain the automatic organizer contact record in the form's immutable notice and existing claim screen; information about data use is not an additional permission prompt. Newsletter and pastoral follow-up remain independently selected purposes. A CRM contact is not permission to send messages, create Organization membership, enroll in a community/course, or infer a religious status.

Additional founder requirement: CRM must show where contacts came from and provide reusable include/exclude filters. Source history and segmentation are part of HC-06 acceptance, not a deferred cosmetic follow-up. The interpretation of a persistent global communication exclusion is being clarified separately; filtering itself does not change consent or delete a contact.

Scope is campaign responses with an eligible HC-07 verified claim, not every account created anywhere in Harvest. Anonymous resource visits and unverified submissions stay on their existing paths. Follow-up/newsletter submissions without an account claim remain pending; introducing an independent verification journey is separate work.

Use a new immutable intake preset/notice contract for this behavior (proposed `connect-intake-v3`, automatic after a verified claim). Existing v1/v2 presets and responses retain their published terms, including HC-07's no-CRM promise; do not silently reinterpret old notices or backfill contacts. The new preset has automatic behavior, not a visitor CRM approval control. Reuse existing version creation/publication and QR lifecycle rules rather than changing a published form in place.

## Visitor and organizer experience

1. The visitor submits the existing separate choices and, if requested, receives the mocked account invitation.
2. Existing email-code authentication and matching-account verification work as today. The claim screen clearly names the organizer and explains that name and verified email will appear in its contact list. No extra CRM choice is shown.
3. The existing claim action completes the verified claim and automatic contact association. Show success only after the database commits. Account-only requests also create/reuse the contact, with no follow-up/newsletter grant added.
4. The personal welcome continues to offer resources/profile/return to the QR page. A minimal authenticated response-rights surface provides withdrawal and response deletion after association; full onboarding/preferences remain HC-08.
5. The contact appears in the existing People list/detail. Authorized organizers see a Source column and a Sources section with campaign, QR/link label, form/version, entry route and date, including additional sources for an existing contact. Provide searchable include/exclude controls and saved views. Response answers and detailed consent evidence remain restricted; source provenance is a minimal operational projection, not a response roster or a generic timeline payload.

New contacts use the submitted preferred name when present, otherwise the existing account's display name, with a bounded verified-email fallback if neither is usable. Email comes from the live verified account and must match the claim recipient. Do not copy unverified phone/social fields, private response text or belief labels. Existing contacts retain their names, notes, identifiers and Organization links; this operation does not overwrite or merge their records.

| Match inside the owning tenant | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No live contact has the verified email | Create one canonical contact and email identifier |
| Exactly one live contact has it, with no conflicting subject binding | Associate the response with that contact |
| Retry of the same completed operation by the same verified subject | Return the same generic success, no second contact, consent projection or event |
| Concurrent contact creation | Serialize/retry the unique-key race and reuse the winner after revalidation |
| Existing subject binding conflicts, identity changed, or a candidate was erased/merged during the operation | Fail closed and show a recoverable generic state; never reveal CRM details or silently relink |
| Same name, unverified address, or matching person in another tenant | No identity match from those facts |

## Persistence and transaction design

Use one additive migration and the established application role, Drizzle metadata and fixed-search-path definer conventions. Preserve every merged migration. Any new tables enable and FORCE RLS, use tenant-composite references, and deny direct application writes.

Proposed additions cover four responsibilities; finalize exact names and columns with the migration review:

- A restricted durable subject binding (`connect_response_subjects`) records which verified user owns rights to one associated response. It does not confer tenant membership, survive response erasure, or depend on a ten-minute invitation row. One binding per tenant/response; contact ownership is reached through the existing response reference. Retain no duplicate email or profile.
- A restricted consent-origin mapping binds each projected canonical consent event to its exact immutable response grant/form/notice and prevents duplicate projection. Add a controlled Connect notice reference to the canonical ledger with corresponding tenant-composite integrity and guard updates. Keep sensitive source linkage outside general CRM-visible evidence strings. Subject actions are recorded honestly, without borrowing an organizer identity.
- A bounded completion outcome tied to the existing hashed browser continuation and verified user supports response-loss retries. Prefer extending existing continuation persistence over another bearer system. It returns only generic success, expires at the original continuation deadline, and cannot authorize a new association or another subject. Persist no raw token or contact address.
- A canonical tenant-scoped source-event ledger (`contact_source_events`) stores minimal provenance for one contact's actual creation/acquisition/touchpoint events. Connect events reference the owning campaign, link and immutable form version with composite integrity and an idempotent response/event key. Record the entry route (`qr` or `short`) from the verified source binding; a QR-route visit is not proof that a physical QR was scanned. Store no raw code, token, destination query, response answer, religious classification or arbitrary payload. Merge re-parents events; response/contact erasure scrubs them through controlled paths. Explicit source kinds support current manual and CSV-import writers as well as Connect; future integrations/forms use the same contract when delivered. Do not invent historical source facts.

Extend the existing claim POST rather than adding a separate approval API. Compose claim creation, association, source event, consent projection, subject binding, completion outcome and `association_consumed_at` in one transaction. An association failure rolls back claim consumption, making a safe retry possible. Authentication/account creation remains independent; a failed association must not erase the account or block free resources.

The trusted server derives the subject from the live session and derives tenant/response from the opaque continuation. Do not accept a caller-selected tenant, response, contact, email, grant status or notice. If a submitted name must be opened from the synthetic intake envelope, decrypt only in the restricted server service, verify its binding, validate it and pass the minimum value into the controlled transaction; never expose the envelope to HTTP.

The database rechecks the live verified account, claim owner/deadline/unconsumed state, source publication/current code/expiry, campaign archive state, response retention, current selected purposes and subject/identifier conflicts. Use wall-clock checks after waits. Adopt and document a total lock order extending receipt → source parents → response → invitation/claim to include canonical contacts and consent. Audit existing CRM edit/merge/erasure lock paths before selecting the order. Preserve try-lock/serialization behavior where an older eraser already holds a response; retry only bounded retryable transaction failures.

Source pause/rotation/archive/expiry blocks a new association while waiting. It must not erase an already committed association or block later withdrawal. No QR lifecycle state or retention deadline changes as a side effect.

## CRM source history and include/exclude filters

### Information and controls

Extend the existing shadcn People toolbar, typed filter model, URL state, saved views, column configuration and contact detail. Use normal labels such as **Source**, **Campaign**, **QR link**, **Form**, **Date added through source**, **Include** and **Exclude**. Present searchable names rather than UUIDs or SQL. Source date differs from the contact's original creation date.

### Filter UI direction — founder requirement

Matei requested a modern, uncluttered interface on 8 November, then supplied [ReUI Filters 10](https://reui.io/components/filters/c-filters-10) as the interaction/layout reference: **one icon-only filter button opens a shadcn popover/dropdown containing the filter builder**. Use the reference's compact rule rows and grouped conditions inside the panel. The toolbar trigger has no visible text on any viewport; do not use “Advanced filters” as a button, panel or menu label. Do not put a permanent wall of fields, a large advanced-search form or separate campaign/QR/form controls across the toolbar.

- Retain the existing full-width People layout and one compact, icon-only `ListFilter` trigger on desktop, tablet and mobile. Use **Filters** for its accessible name and hover/focus tooltip. Put any active count in the open panel/result summary rather than adding a text label to the trigger. The trigger and all interactive targets remain at least 44px.
- Opening the trigger reveals one anchored dropdown-style panel. Reuse the existing shadcn `Popover` + `Command` filter-builder pattern for searchable field selection and form controls; use shadcn menu primitives where appropriate rather than hand-building a dropdown. The result must look and behave as one filter dropdown, not multiple competing toolbar controls.
- Show a search box and small groups such as **Source**, **Contact details**, **Contact permissions** and **Custom fields**. All authorized filters are reachable here; search and a bounded scroll area keep a long catalog manageable. Hide unavailable capabilities instead of displaying dead options.
- Selecting a field reveals only its relevant include/exclude/operator/value controls inside the same panel, with a clear Back action. Campaign and link pickers use readable names and search. Avoid stacks of nested fly-out menus, raw query syntax and showing every possible condition at once. Keep correlated source conditions together as one readable source rule.
- Stage changes until **Apply filters**. Keep **Cancel** and **Clear all** easy to find; Cancel, Escape and outside dismissal leave the applied result set unchanged. Show loading/error/empty states in place. The panel must not jump, overflow the viewport or reset unrelated filters when a value is unavailable.
- After applying, close the panel and show only compact, editable/removable active-filter chips and the matching result count. Summarize many chips with **+N more** rather than letting them take over the page; provide Clear all without opening a second panel. Saved views stay in the existing view control.
- Use theme tokens, restrained borders, consistent spacing and typography, a clear selected state and subtle motion that respects reduced-motion settings. Avoid decorative gradients, excessive cards or color-coded logic that requires explanation. Both light and dark themes must be equally readable.
- On mobile, the same dropdown uses the available viewport width, bounded height and internal scrolling with reachable actions; retain one trigger and no horizontal scrolling. Keyboard opening, search/selection, Back, Apply, Escape and return focus must be explicit acceptance checks. Use suitable popover/dialog and combobox semantics for editable controls; do not apply menu roles to arbitrary form fields.

UI acceptance: from a clean CRM list, a non-technical organizer can open the filter icon, find Campaign, choose Include A and Exclude B, apply, understand the resulting chips/count, edit a rule and clear it without reading implementation terms. Capture closed, field-search, condition-editing, applied-summary and empty-result states on mobile/tablet/desktop in both themes. Verify the trigger remains icon-only and “Advanced filters” does not appear. Adapt the reference's presentation to Harvest's existing components and server-validated query contract; its demo client-side matching/counting is not authoritative for CRM results.

- Filter by source kind (Connect, manual entry, CSV import, and only other kinds with real writers), one or more campaigns, named QR/short links, form/version and source-date range. Distinguish any recorded source, first recorded source and most recent recorded source. Keep all sources rather than overwriting one `source` field on the person.
- Compose source rules with existing name/email/phone/Organization/custom-field/date filters. Add effective newsletter and follow-up permission filters; the existing Marketing filter must not misrepresent these distinct purposes. None/unknown must be selectable explicitly.
- Record new manual/import creation and import associations through their real writers. Existing contacts without trustworthy acquisition evidence display **Unknown**, not an invented Manual source. Adding a Connect association to an existing contact adds a source event and does not relabel it as newly created by Connect. Never reconstruct anonymous browsing or infer sensitive attributes.
- Save named views such as “Welcome Evening contacts”, “Newsletter subscribers from campaign A”, and “All contacts except campaign B”. No second saved-view or audience system is introduced.

### Exact matching rules

Use one bounded, versioned source-rule structure alongside existing CRM filters rather than flattening campaign and QR predicates into unrelated events. Default semantics: match any inclusion group, then subtract every contact matching any exclusion group. Other ordinary filters combine with this result using AND. Each source group matches campaign/link/form/date on the **same** source event. Labels and help must explain the selected semantics; no arbitrary recursive query builder is required.

| Example | Required result |
| --- | --- |
| Include campaigns A or B | Union of matching contacts, each person once |
| Include A; exclude B | Contacts with an A event and no B event, even if one person has both |
| Campaign A + QR 2 in one source group | Both conditions must match the same event; separate A and QR 2 events do not qualify |
| Exclude source B | `NOT EXISTS` a matching B event; a different source does not let that contact slip through |
| No include rules | All otherwise eligible contacts, minus exclusions |
| Unknown source | No retained provenance; never guessed from the person's name, email or missing data |

Apply the same validated server predicate to result rows, counts, pagination, export preview and export file. Preserve state through reload/navigation and saved-view edits. Use `EXISTS` / `NOT EXISTS` so several source events do not duplicate contacts or inflate counts. “Select all matching” must mean the filtered set wherever a bulk selector exists; a filter must never quietly become all contacts.

Version the serializable view schema additively; old views retain their existing meaning. Extend URL state detection, saved-view validation, option resolution and export parsing together. Unknown/foreign or unauthorized source IDs and invalid/revoked source rules fail closed for source-segment results and exports; do not fall back silently to an unfiltered view. Archived sources remain identifiable by stable ID and an archived label while their provenance is retained. Deleting or hiding a source must not silently drop an exclusion clause.

### Access, retention and downstream use

Source names can reveal campaign participation. Default exact Connect source visibility/filtering/export to owner/admin through a specific checked capability, aligned with the existing restricted Connect boundary. Ordinary CRM permission alone must not bypass it through hidden columns, URLs, counts, shared saved views, exports or AI queries. Recheck live access server-side; sharing a view does not grant its creator's source access. Scope all source pickers and predicates to the owning tenant. No new role-management UI is required in this slice.

Allow authorized CSV exports to use source filters and explicitly selected minimal source columns; exclude answers, tokens and detailed evidence. AI tools/webhook payloads receive no new source projection in HC-06. Source filters prepare a reusable selection contract for future newsletters, campaigns and automations; those modules must apply it and recheck consent/rights when they are implemented. HC-06 does not claim nonexistent senders or automation audiences are wired.

Filtering is selection, not authorization: including a person cannot restore a withdrawn purpose, authorize delivery or bypass a future suppression rule. Excluding a person from a view does not erase them, rewrite their origins or unsubscribe them. A separate persistent Do not contact rule, if requested, needs its own explicit state and enforcement contract.

Source history follows the associated response's existing expiry/erasure boundary in this bounded implementation; do not silently retain source facts indefinitely after scrubbing their response. Record the resulting Unknown/no-retained-source behavior in UI/tests. Production source/CRM retention remains part of the outstanding activation review.

### Consent projection

- CRM creation for an account-only claim adds no newsletter, marketing or follow-up grant. Invitation/account permission remains account-scoped rather than permission for organizer messaging. Automatic association requires the new form/notice contract; old v1/v2 claims remain on their original behavior.
- Project only currently effective, explicitly selected operational purposes (`newsletter`, `outreach_follow_up`), retaining original occurrence time and exact immutable notice provenance. Do not map newsletter to generic marketing or follow-up to `form_follow_up` merely to fit an unrelated API.
- Reuse the CRM ledger's withdrawal precedence on both clocks. Importing an older response grant must not revive a later CRM withdrawal or unknown state. Never make an old grant newer by assigning the association time as its occurrence time.
- Preserve append-only evidence; a response and a CRM withdrawal for the same channel/purpose must agree before any future sender/assigner may act. No sender is enabled in HC-06.

### Withdrawal, erasure, expiry and merge

These are required in HC-06, not deferred merely because HC-14 expands rights later:

- Carry receipt-authorized response rights across pending → associated. A receipt may revoke the purposes granted by its response or erase that response, but cannot read CRM data or erase an unrelated/pre-existing contact/account. Do not upgrade a revocation receipt into an identity claim.
- Use the durable subject binding for authenticated, response-scoped rights after the short claim expires. Derive scope from an opaque subject-owned handle and session; no arbitrary workspace access. Apply same-origin, bounded-body, no-store/no-referrer protections.
- Propagate response-purpose withdrawal into the canonical ledger without granting anything else. Revoke only that request's evidence; ensure an applicable later withdrawal cannot be bypassed by another stale response projection. Preserve other independent purposes.
- Response erasure scrubs its subject binding, source ledger/projection, consent-origin mapping, receipt/encrypted envelope, claims/continuations/outcomes and identifying audit/outbox/job/replay references. It does not delete an independently existing CRM person or the global account. Canonical contact erasure continues to scrub all of its associated responses, source events and consent evidence.
- Preserve the original response expiry when associating and include associated response evidence in bounded expiry cleanup. Do not silently turn pending retention into indefinite response storage. This does not choose production CRM retention durations or approve retaining account-only contact records; those remain activation-policy work.
- Existing explicit owner/admin CRM merge must move an associated response and its source history to the kept contact atomically, preserve subject rights/evidence and then erase the source contact. Preserve every distinct origin, deduplicate replay events, and recompute first/latest source deterministically. Update preview counts and erasure integration as necessary; do not let merge delete the response accidentally through the source contact's eraser. Association itself never invokes merge.
- Coordinate account deletion with subject binding cleanup and existing rights procedures; deleting a global user must not leave an accessible orphan capability. Do not equate account deletion with permission to delete a pre-existing organizer contact.

## Verification and exit criteria

- Two-tenant UI/API/direct SQL isolation; forged parent/subject/contact/notice references; no direct app writes; live organizer role removal for staff rights and live subject verification/account changes for subject operations. No organizer membership required or granted for a verified guest claim.
- New/existing/account-only claims under the new preset; all independent choice combinations; invitation-only creates a contact with no operational grants. - Same-response retry, lost success response, parallel claims, different responses sharing a verified email, concurrent manual creation/edit/merge, unique-key contention and no overwrite of existing contacts. No name-based/global identity matching.
- Multi-source union/exclusion, same-event correlation, first/latest/unknown history, duplicate-free counts/pagination, no inclusion rules, all-excluded empty states, archived/deleted sources, old/new saved-view/URL round trips, matching export preview/file and source columns. Include source permission downgrade, shared-view access, forged tenant IDs and count/exclusion inference probes; invalid source rules must not broaden results.
- Manual/import/Connect provenance writers, existing-contact additional origins, replay dedupe, merge history and expiry/erasure cleanup. Effective newsletter/follow-up filters must remain distinct from marketing and must follow withdrawal precedence. Verify future-facing selection contracts without claiming unimplemented outbound enforcement.
- Lock-wait races against withdrawal, erasure, retention expiry, source pause/rotation/archive/expiry, account changes and claim expiry. All-or-nothing state and truthful success; duplicate publication-style side effects must not occur.
- Original Connect notice provenance, exact-purpose projection, no grant resurrection after later CRM withdrawal/unknown, receipt rights after association, authenticated rights after claim expiry, contact/response/account deletion boundaries, merge survival and absence of identifying residues in durable payloads/exports.
- Existing Connect/auth/landing regressions, including consent choices, claim continuation/mismatch/switch/decline, withdrawal/erasure, free guest access, private landing drafts and published QR lifecycle.
- Mobile/tablet/desktop in both themes; keyboard/focus ordering, axe, overflow, 44px controls and ordinary screenshot content. Check all newly changed states, including automatic-success, recoverable conflict and response-rights screens.
- Lint, typecheck, unit/integration/RLS suites, build, migration metadata checks and fresh/populated upgrade/replay. Run relevant PWA/security checks if shared route/bootstrap/cache behavior changes. Record actual counts and execution provenance, not planned passing results.
