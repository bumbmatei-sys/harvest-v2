## CRM reports decisions (founder, 22 Nov)

Approved for the CRM reports slice (`docs/acceptance/crm-reports.md`); they settle four rows of `docs/plans/crm-leftovers.md`.

- **Pipeline conversion:** deals won divided by deals created in the period. The cohort is the deals created in the period; a reopened deal counts by its original creation; archived deals are excluded from numerator and denominator; the period timestamp is the deal's creation time. Stage-to-stage movement is not reported (deferred).
- **Follow-up task throughput and overdue:** the population is CRM tasks (Connect follow-up cases are not mixed in, and the report says so). Overdue is a live snapshot of open tasks past due now, not history. Throughput is tasks completed in the period, and a reopened task counts again when re-completed.
- **Source performance and contact growth:** the first source gets the credit. The outcome per source is contacts created plus how many have an open or won deal. Growth measures additions, not net change; erased contacts are gone and a merged pair counts once.
- **Report access and disclosure:** owners and admins see ministry totals; members see only their own work (own tasks and deals); viewers see nothing. The time zone is the ministry's if the schema has one, otherwise UTC, and the report footer says which. Periods: last 7, 30 and 90 days, this month and last month. Any cohort below five is suppressed, protected against subtraction across totals, overlapping periods and filters (strategy in the acceptance record). Reports are read-only aggregates: no row-level personal data, no export of suppressed cells, nothing sent out.
