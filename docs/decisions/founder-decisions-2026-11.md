## 4. Communities

- **Chat (C11) decisions (17 Nov):** polling first; admin per-community setting for member-to-member DMs (on or off); admins can always start a conversation with any member and the member can reply while a current host has engaged; admins cannot read member-to-member DMs; members can opt out; when DMs are switched off, member-to-member chats become read-only and staff-started chats stay two-way; message retention until deleted then 30-day recovery; reported-DM evidence kept 180 days after the decision, subject to legal review; moderators have no DM powers; attachments deferred until a malware scanner exists. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-communities-c11-chat-design.md`.
- **Profile and events (17 Nov):** Skool-style member profile page and events with RSVP are part of Communities completion.
- **Live (livestream) decisions (16 Nov):** any member of the community can watch the stream (members only, not a public link); the admin adds a YouTube live URL and Harvest embeds it; live comments start as near-live polling; prayer requests: the author chooses anonymous or named, and anonymous hides the author from other members while the prayer team and admins and moderators can still see them (audited, with a clear notice at submit); prayer team in V1 is owner, admin and moderator; prayer retention 30 days after closed, maximum 90 days after submission, 180 days for prayer-wall items, subject to legal review; replay set explicitly by the admin with a notice that the raw video stays outside Harvest's control (use an unlisted live); adult-accounts policy plus legal review before activation; donate-live slot reserved and hidden until the founder supplies the workaround; moderated from the admin control room; Operations lists live events across communities. Other nine questions use recommended defaults, not yet confirmed.
