## Activation prerequisites outside the five decisions

Independent reviews of HC-DEC1b and HC-13 found gaps that are not one of the five decisions above but still stand between the current code and real data. Reconciled 16 Nov 2026. **27 Nov 2026:** the founder approved the policies for CRM contact visibility, audit retention and denial logging ([policy pack §9](hc-02-policy-pack.md#9-gaps-outside-the-five-gates-that-block-real-data)); the items below marked "not built" are still not built, and none of them is activated.

- **CRM contact visibility: not built; policy approved 27 Nov (pack §9), not activated.** Every ministry role, viewers included, can read every contact and every consent row, including the evidence `source` and `evidence` text. A case or a counselor assignment limits what is learned about the *case*, not about the *person*. A restricted-visibility decision (who may read which contacts, consent evidence and sources) is needed before real data.
- **Withdrawal propagation into cases (HC-14): built; behaviour covered by the Gate 1 policy approved 27 Nov; activation UNAPPROVED, not activated.** Migration 0052 moves affected non-terminal cases to `withdrawn` in the withdrawal transaction, clears assignments and writes a system audit. It reconciles pre-existing withdrawals and preserves erasure, merge and terminal-state rules. See [HC-14 acceptance](../acceptance/hc-14.md). Production migrations 0031+ remain unapplied; production collection and delivery stay hard-disabled.
- **Campaign aggregate metrics (HC-15): built; small-group suppression covered by the Gate 3 policy approved 27 Nov; activation UNAPPROVED, not activated.** Migration 0054 provides owner/admin-only suppressed campaign counts and day-level scan counters with synthetic-only capture. No person-level scan events or raw aggregate totals are exposed. See [HC-15 acceptance](../acceptance/hc-15.md); no gate is activated and no real collection is approved.
- **Audit retention period, denial and security-event logging: not built; policy approved 27 Nov (pack §9: 12 months, route and reason code only), not activated.** No retention period is enforced on audit rows yet; denied attempts (403) are not logged. The [staff rights-request runbook](../runbooks/connect-rights-requests.md) is documentation only and not operationally verified; it does not enable a production rights workflow (the pack §7 rights-request steps were approved as policy on 27 Nov, and the rights-request register is not built).
